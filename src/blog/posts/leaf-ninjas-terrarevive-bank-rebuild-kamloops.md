Some of the best work happening in restoration right now is being done by crews like Leaf Ninjas. They're based out of Rocky View County, Alberta, and they do riparian work, slopes, native plantings, overseeding and bioengineering. On a bioengineering job they build live plant material right into the bank, so the plants end up holding the bank together.

Their goal is simple. Get native plants to survive and establish as fast as possible on ground that's been torn up. Brett Schritt, Senior Project Manager at Leaf Ninjas, put it like this:

> "Any time we can find something that even increases survivability, growth, speed of establishment etc by 10% we jump at it."

That's a high bar, and it's the bar they set for anything they bring onto a site. So we were pretty happy when they started putting TerraRevive in the ground with their plants.

## The problem with stripped ground

Banks and slopes that have been stripped or rebuilt don't give plants much to work with. There's almost no organic matter and not much soil biology left. You can put good plant material in, but if the soil can't support it, survival and establishment suffer.

That's exactly what TerraRevive is built for. It's an organic pellet that puts organic matter back into the soil and feeds the soil biology that helps plants get going. It's all about soil health.

## Three ways in, depending on the job

Leaf Ninjas use TerraRevive three different ways, so it's working right where the roots are:

- **Native plantings.** Top dressed around each planted native, under the mulch.
- **Overseeding.** Spread with the seed. In Brett's words, it's there "to have something puff up and provide nutrients and moisture retention for germination."
- **Bioengineering.** Laid in layers with the propagules while they rebuild the bank profile.

## The bank rebuild near Kamloops

The photos in this post are from one large bioengineering bank rebuild near Kamloops, BC. The bank was rebuilt in layers in spring 2025, with TerraRevive going in alongside the live cuttings as each layer went up. When construction wrapped, it was a bare, freshly built bank with every layer built and matted.
