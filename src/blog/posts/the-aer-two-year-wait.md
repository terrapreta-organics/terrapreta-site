If you're reclaiming a well site or a pipeline right-of-way in Alberta, the AER sets the clock. Organic amendments need the landowner's written OK and have to be recorded. The vegetation assessment waits at least two years after the last application.

## Where TerraRevive fits

TerraRevive is an 8.5-2-1 dry pellet for stripped and disturbed soil, with rates starting at 1,500 lb/ac. It doesn't replace topsoil. It builds soil health in the ground you've got, with slow-release nitrogen, organic matter and food for soil biology.
