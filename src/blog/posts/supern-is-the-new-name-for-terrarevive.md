SuperN is our organic nitrogen pellet for hay and crop ground.

## What SuperN is

SuperN is an organic nitrogen pellet. Soil biology releases the nitrogen over time, rather than all at once. The guaranteed analysis is 8.5-2-1.

## Where it's made

We make it at our plant in Sundre, Alberta. It ships in 1,500 lb totes, with Western Canada delivery or pickup in Sundre.

## How much to put down

Every field is different, so we set your rate when we quote. Every quote comes with a rate recommendation.
